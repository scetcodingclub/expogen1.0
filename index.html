What is EXPOGEN 1.0?

EXPOGEN 1.0 is a project expo organized by the Nexus Coding Club at Shadan College of Engineering and Technology. The event serves as a platform for students to showcase their projects, foster collaboration, and exchange ideas in a dynamic, interactive environment.

Participants will have the opportunity to interact directly with attendees, demonstrate their projects, and engage in discussions about the potential impact of their innovations. The event aims to provide an immersive, hands-on experience, allowing attendees to connect with fellow students, faculty.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can participate?

EXPOGEN 1.0 is open to all students of Shadan College of Engineering and Technology.

Can I present an incomplete project?

Yes, functional, non-functional, and incomplete projects are allowed.

How long should my presentation be?

Presentations should last between 5 to 10 minutes.

What do I need to bring for my booth?

Bring your project, equipment (laptop, posters, etc.), and anything to set up and decorate your booth.

Is there a specific format for the project?

No specific format, but you should present your project using demos and visual aids like slides or posters.

Will there be judging or awards?

No, this edition does not have judging or awards. All participants will receive certificates for participation.

Can I present as a team?

Yes, you can present individually or with a team.

Is there a dress code?

No, but dress professionally for the event.
